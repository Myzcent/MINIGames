# 🌻 FarmVille 3D: Sunny Meadow Farm

A charming, modern **3D FarmVille & Farming Simulator** built with **Next.js 16**, **React 19**, and **Three.js**.

---

## 🎮 How to Play & Invite Friends (Same WiFi)

### 1. Play on Your Computer:
👉 **[http://localhost:3030](http://localhost:3030)**

### 2. Invite Friends on the Same WiFi:
Give your friends this local network address:
👉 **`http://10.200.54.200:3030`**

- Anyone connected to your **same WiFi network** can open **`http://10.200.54.200:3030`** on their:
  - 📱 Mobile Phone (iPhone / Android)
  - 📱 iPad / Tablet
  - 💻 Laptop / PC
- **Real-time Co-op**: You will see each other's 3D Farmer characters walking around with their names, plowing, watering, and harvesting the same farmland together in real-time!

## 🌾 Features & Gameplay

1. **3D Farm Meadow & Buildings**:
   - **Classic Red Barn** with white trim & rooster weather vane.
   - **Spinning Rustic Windmill** with animated cloth lattice sails.
   - **Country Farmhouse** with stone chimney puffing stylized white smoke puffs.
   - **Wooden Silo** for grain & harvest storage.
   - **Stone Water Well** with sparkling blue water.
   - **Apple Orchard** with harvestable sweet red apples.
   - **Fenced Pasture** with roaming 3D livestock (Chickens, Cows, Sheep).
   - **Animated Nature**: Fluttering colorful butterflies, flower meadows, and ambient sunny dust particles.

2. **Interactive 3D Farming Grid (6x6 = 36 Plots)**:
   - ⛏️ **Plow (Hoe)**: Till lush green grass into dark, rich planting soil.
   - 🌱 **Plant Seeds**: Choose from 7 diverse crops (Wheat 🌾, Carrot 🥕, Tomato 🍅, Strawberry 🍓, Corn 🌽, Pumpkin 🎃, Sunflower 🌻).
   - 💧 **Water Can**: Water your plots to accelerate growth speed by **2x**!
   - 🧺 **Harvest (Scythe)**: When crops are ripe (bouncing golden harvest badge), collect them for coins, EXP, and stored produce!
   - 🧪 **Speed-Gro Fertilizer**: Instantly mature any growing crop!

3. **Farm Livestock & Ranching**:
   - 🐔 **Clucky Hen**: Lays fresh farm eggs 🥚 every 18 seconds.
   - 🐄 **Daisy Cow**: Produces rich bottles of milk 🥛 every 35 seconds.
   - 🐑 **Fluffy Sheep**: Produces spun white wool 🧶 every 55 seconds.
   - Bouncing alert bubbles appear over their heads when ready for collection!

4. **Economy, Market & Orders**:
   - 🛒 **Market Stand**: Buy seeds in stacks of 5 or 10, or adopt new farm animals.
   - 📦 **Grain Silo & Storage Barn**: View all harvested crops and produce; sell individually or click **"Sell All"** for instant coin windfalls.
   - 📋 **Order Board**: Complete customer delivery tickets (Grandma Daisy, Chef Pierre, Sweet Lily, Mayor Higgins) for huge coin, EXP, and gem rewards!

5. **Audio & Ambience**:
   - Web Audio API synthesizer for earthy hoeing, seed sprinkling, water splashing, harvest pops, animal calls, and jingling coins.
   - 🎵 Toggleable pleasant acoustic farm background melody!

## 🕹️ Controls

| Control | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Left Click** on Plot / Animal | Plow, Plant, Water, Harvest, or Collect |
| **Right Click + Drag** (or **Shift + Drag**) | Pan Camera across farmland |
| **Mouse Wheel** | Zoom Camera In / Out |
| **Toolbar Dock** (Bottom) | Switch tools: Select, Hoe, Plant, Water, Harvest, Speed-Gro |
| **Side Ribbon** (Right) | Open Market 🛒, Silo 📦, Orders 📋, or Help ❓ |
